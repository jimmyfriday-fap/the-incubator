This plan is built one step at a time, each step in its own session. The plan below holds only your step; the other steps are listed by title and belong to other sessions. Earlier steps are already committed on this branch.

1. Build only your step. Do not review, redo or re-read earlier steps' work unless your step names a file they wrote.
2. The notes from earlier steps are below (when there are any). They hold what you need from them: use them instead of re-reading.
3. Your step may already be partly done by a session that stopped at a limit. Run `git status` and `git diff` (if open to you) before you write anything.
